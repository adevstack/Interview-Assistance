Interview Tips
{% if session.category == 'warmup' %}
  • Be concise but thorough
  • Highlight relevant aspects of your background
  • Show enthusiasm for the role
  • Connect your answer to the position you're applying for
{% elif session.category == 'technical' %}
  • Start by explaining your understanding of the concept
  • Use specific examples from your experience
  • Discuss pros and cons when applicable
  • Break down complex problems into smaller steps
  • Think out loud to show your problem-solving process
{% elif session.category == 'behavioral' %}
  • Situation: Describe the context and background
  • Task: Explain your responsibility in that situation
  • Action: Detail the specific actions you took
  • Result: Share the outcomes and what you learned
  • Use specific metrics when possible to quantify your impact
{% elif session.category == 'situational' %}
  • Consider multiple approaches to the scenario
  • Explain your decision-making process
  • Demonstrate problem-solving and critical thinking
  • Show how you would communicate with stakeholders
  • Discuss how you would measure success
{% endif %}
